Our Story

More than a café, MOON is a little escape where good coffee, garden calm and meaningful moments come together.

The idea behind MOON

A little escape under the moon.

MOON – The Dreamy Garden Café is imagined as a peaceful hideaway from the rush of everyday life. Inspired by warm evenings, natural textures and the charm of a garden gathering, our space invites you to slow down and enjoy the moment.

Whether you arrive for your morning coffee, an unhurried catch-up with friends or a special evening with someone you love, we want every visit to feel welcoming, thoughtful and memorable.
