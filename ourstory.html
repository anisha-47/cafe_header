OUR JOURNEY

From a Dream to MOON

MOON was born from a simple dream — to create a place where people can relax, connect and enjoy life's little pleasures. We wanted to build more than just another cafe; we wanted to create a peaceful escape where every guest feels welcome.

Nestled in a beautiful garden, our cafe brings together delicious food, freshly brewed coffee and a warm atmosphere. From cozy conversations to memorable celebrations, every corner is designed to make your moments special.
